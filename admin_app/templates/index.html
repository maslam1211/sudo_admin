About SudoTag

SudoTag is a smart vehicle identification and private communication platform. Physical QR tags connect scanners to owners—without exposing personal phone numbers.

  • Scan a SudoTag or plate to notify, chat, SMS, or call the owner privately.
  • Owners manage vehicles, documents, family access, Live Status, and Lost Mode in the app.
  • Activate on web or in-app, order tags online, and stay protected with privacy-first channels.

Available on Google Play and the App Store. Use the website to find a vehicle, activate a new QR, or buy a SudoTag—then manage everything from the mobile app.

Private scan-to-contact

Scan a SudoTag QR or plate on web or in-app to reach the owner—without revealing anyone’s personal number.

Owner privacy & Live Status

Control push, chat, SMS, and voice channels. Show Available, Busy, Driving, Do Not Disturb, and more.

Lost Mode recovery

Enable Lost Mode per vehicle. Community tips and location sharing help you recover faster.

Masked chat, SMS & voice

Communicate through SudoTag channels—chat, templated SMS, and routed voice—with emergency options when needed.
