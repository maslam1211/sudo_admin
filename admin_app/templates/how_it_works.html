Getting Started

Three Ways to Activate SudoTag

Whether you're ordering a new QR, activating one you already have, or setting up via the web — we've got you covered.

1

Order a SudoTag QR

Don't have a SudoTag QR yet? Order one from the website or the app and track delivery to your door.

Buy Now on Website

1

Download the SudoTag App

Get the app from Google Play or the App Store on your Android or iOS device.

2

Create an Account or Sign In

Register with your details or log in if you already have a SudoTag account.

3

Register Your Vehicle

Complete your vehicle profile with the required details for identification.

4

Tap Order QR & Pay

Place your order and complete the secure payment process within the app.

5

Track Your Order

Monitor order status anytime from the Orders section in the app.

6

Receive Your SudoTag QR

Your physical SudoTag QR arrives at your registered address — ready to activate.

Order Tracking

After placing an order, view these details directly from the Orders section in the SudoTag App:

Order Status
Payment Status
Processing Updates
Shipping Information
Delivery Status
2

Activate Through the App

Already purchased a SudoTag QR? Link it to your vehicle profile in minutes.

1

Open the SudoTag App & Log In

Launch the app and sign in to your account.

2

Select Activate QR

From your dashboard, tap the Activate QR option.

3

Tap Scan QR

Open the camera scanner to read your SudoTag QR code.

4

Scan Your SudoTag QR Code

Point your camera at the QR sticker on your SudoTag product.

5

Complete Vehicle & Owner Info

Fill in your vehicle details and owner information.

6

Add Emergency Contacts

Optionally add emergency contact details for urgent situations.

7

Submit & Activate

Submit the activation form — your QR is linked to your vehicle profile and activated successfully.

3

Activate Through the Web

No app required to get started — activate your QR directly from your smartphone browser.

1

Scan the QR Code

Use your smartphone camera to scan the SudoTag QR code.

2

Open the Activation Page

The SudoTag activation page opens automatically in your browser.

3

Complete Activation

Fill in the required vehicle and owner details on the web form.

4

Account Created Automatically

A SudoTag account is created for you during the process.

5

Check Your Email

Login details and a temporary password are sent to your registered email address.

6

Download the App & Sign In

Get the SudoTag app and sign in with your new credentials.

7

Update Password & Go Live

Change your password after first login — your vehicle profile is now active and ready to manage.
