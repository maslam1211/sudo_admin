Activate in the mobile app

Best if you already use SudoTag on Android or iPhone.

  1. 1
    Open SudoTag & sign in Download from Google Play or App Store if needed.
  2. 2
    Tap Activate QR From your home / dashboard, choose Activate QR.
  3. 3
    Scan your sticker Point the camera at the SudoTag QR (or pick from gallery).
  4. 4
    Enter vehicle details Registration number, vehicle info, and owner details.
  5. 5
    Submit — QR is active Optional: add emergency contacts, then finish.

Activate on phone browser

No app install required to activate — use Safari / Chrome on your phone.

  1. 1
    Open Scan QR on the website Or scan the sticker with your phone camera — the link opens SudoTag.
  2. 2
    Scan or paste the QR Use camera, gallery, or paste the QR link / ID.
  3. 3
    If QR is new → activation form Inactive tags open activate. Already active tags open notify.
  4. 4
    Fill vehicle & owner info Complete the form on your mobile browser and submit.
  5. 5
    Check email & open the app Login details are sent by email. Download the app to manage your tag.
Activate on Mobile Web
