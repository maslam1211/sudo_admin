Help

Good to know before you tag.

Can people who scan my tag see my phone number?

No. They reach you through SudoTag's private channels (notify, chat, SMS or masked call), and your personal number is never shown to them.

How do I activate my SudoTag?

Use Scan & Find on this page or in the app. Scan your new sticker, verify your mobile number with an OTP and add your vehicle details. Your login details are shown on screen and emailed to you.

How do I contact a vehicle's owner?

Scan the tag with your phone camera, or search the number plate. Pick notify, chat, SMS or masked call. You don't need to install anything.

Does the tag need a battery or charging?

No. SudoTag is a weatherproof QR sticker with nothing to charge or replace. All the smarts live in the app.

What if someone else is driving my vehicle?

Family and temporary assign is coming soon. You'll be able to send alerts to the person driving for the time you choose, and they'll come back to you afterwards.

What is Lost Mode? Soon

Lost Mode is coming soon. It will mark a vehicle as missing, so when someone scans it they can send you tips and their location to help you recover it faster.

Does it work for bikes, autos and trucks?

Yes. One tag works on any vehicle: car, bike, scooter, auto-rickshaw, taxi, truck or bus. Tools to manage a whole fleet together are coming soon.

How do I order a tag?

Tap Buy SudoTag to order official stickers online. When it arrives, stick it on and activate it here or in the app.
